# Site rules

The site is the shell of spec clean-site around the canvas cemetery: a one-row header, Home (the headline, the chips, the Scene or the List, the Footer bar and the Panel), a tabbed Drawer, a Replay told step by step, a Guide page, a Legend, a Tour, failure cards and a Track slot for music. The design the owner approved on 26 November 2026 is the reference for look, layout and wording; real data wins over it, and the layout stays.

## One thing at a time

- The screen state (load/screen-state.ts, held by load/use-view.ts) is the one owner of what is open: at most one View owner (the Drawer, a Replay, the Guide page or the Tour) and at most one Pop-over on top of it (the example list, the Legend, the attack picker or a Term meaning). Every overlay renders from it and from nothing else. Opening a View owner closes the one before it and any Pop-over; opening a Pop-over closes the other Pop-over and keeps the View owner.
- Nothing floats over a grave. On Home no visible DOM element sits over a grave's Art box but the grave's own transparent button and its focus outline, the scanning and failure cards, the Drawer and its scrim, the Tour's dimming, card and spotlight, an open Pop-over, and the sticky header when the page scrolls under it. The help line and the date badge sit in the Scene's sky band (scene/overlay-band.ts), clear of every Art box. e2e/clean.spec.ts samples this in the browser at rest, on hover, with a filter, at an earlier date, with each Pop-over open, in the Drawer, under the Tour, with the Welcome bar and after a Live scan.
- The details of a grave show in the Panel (panel/Inspector.tsx), never in a tooltip or a bubble. The Inspector shows the grave under the pointer, else the one whose button has focus, else the Selected grave, and "Selected" beside its heading then. At the scan date it shows the Epitaph line of the reason's family under the name, plain Inter in the muted colour, with no Term marked; at an earlier date or for a reason with no family, none. The Fix order (panel/fix-order.ts) lists up to five Possessed, Dead and Haunted packages in triage order.
- Nothing moves when the grave changes (3.26): the Inspector, the Fix order card and the Step panel keep one height per layout (Wide 457, 261 and 476 px; Middle 416, 261 and 386 px; Narrow 514, 351 and 525 px), their lines are clamped with an ellipsis through panel/Clamped.tsx, which keeps the whole text in the DOM and as the title while cut, and the Home grid aligns its columns to the top so a taller Panel never stretches the Scene card. e2e/clean.spec.ts checks every box at 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844 on hover, select, the List and every Replay Step.
- Escape closes an open Pop-over, else ends the Tour, else closes the Drawer, else takes a Replay or the Guide page home. The wordmark, "← Cemetery" on a Replay or the Guide page, "Exit replay", "Back to the cemetery" and "Go to the cemetery" take the Home action: Home of the scan shown before, at its scan date, with no filter, Drawer, Tour or Pop-over, and the Graveyard or List choice kept. The Drawer's scrim covers the header, as in the approved design; its breadcrumb, close button, the scrim and Escape close it.

## The header and Home

- The header is one row of at most 64 px from 700 px up: the wordmark link, the scan tag ("Sample scan", the owner/name of a Live scan, "Replay" or "Scan file", hidden under 1300 px), the Scan field with its Scan button, then "What is Sexton?", Legend, Music (shown, since a track is set; it hides only if the slot is emptied) and Tour. Under 1100 px Legend, Music and Tour show icons only, each with its accessible name; under 700 px the header wraps with the Scan field on its own row. The gold accent marks only the Scan button, "What is Sexton?", the wordmark glyph, the scan tag, the field's focus border, a pressed Music button and focus outlines. The Fixture name (sexton-demo/fixture) never shows.
- The Scan field takes owner/name or a github.com repository link through header/repo-input.ts; anything else marks the field, keeps focus and sends nothing. Its example list ("Try one") opens on focus. With no API base the field and button are disabled, described by "Scanning is off in this build." While a Live scan loads, the scanning card shows over the Scene, with no progress bar, and the Scanning line under its note.
- The Welcome bar sits in the page flow under the header until it is closed or the Guide page opens, with the Guide ghost at its start; sexton:welcome = "closed" is stored where storage allows (guide/welcome-pref.ts).
- Home: the headline counts the Possessed packages at the shown date ("<n> of <total> dependencies need you now."); with none at the scan date it names the Dead and Haunted ones ("Nothing is possessed. <k> of <total> need fixing."), else a failing policy ("Nothing is possessed. The policy fails on <r> rules."), and reads "Nothing needs you now." only when neither applies (home/headline.ts, words in copy/headline.ts); the notices of a missing lockfile, packages resolved from ranges and a capped scan sit under it, then the five State chips and the Graveyard / List switch. A pressed chip draws the other States' graves at one fixed dim alpha (DIM_ALPHA_BYTE, in save and restore around each grave) and takes their buttons out of the tab order; the arrow keys still reach every grave. The grave under the pointer gets the hover ring in ringHover on the Ring casing; the Selected grave gets the selection ring.
- Left and Right move the Selected grave in triage order, wrapping, from the page body, a grave button or a Fix order row while nothing is open; Enter on the body opens its Drawer. A Fix order row selects and opens nothing.
- The List (home/ListView.tsx) replaces the Scene: Package, Version, State, Why and Fix, "Find a package", sorts by name or worst first with aria-sort, the chip filter, "No package matches." and a row press or Enter opening the Drawer. It shows the State at the shown date. 300 packages show each Find key's rows within 100 ms (e2e/list.spec.ts).
- The Footer bar (footer.foot-bar) under the Scene holds the History label, the native range "History date" with aria-valuetext, its start label and "Today, <scan date>", "Back to now" at an earlier date, or the too-large line, and "Watch a real attack", which opens the attack picker. The site footer holds the scan time in a <time> element, the policy line and the evidence links "Evidence JSON" and "Evidence Markdown", and the track's one-line credit when one is set ("Music: Ghost Party by The Piano Says, CC BY 3.0", its title and licence linked). At 1440 by 900 Home fits the viewport with no page scroll, with the Welcome bar open or closed.
- Layouts: from 1100 px the Panel is 372 px on the right of the Scene; under 1100 px the page is one column (headline and chips, the Scene, the Footer bar, the Panel). The Scene is a card with the page gutter around it (28 px, 14 px under 700 px). At 360 px nothing scrolls sideways, with the Drawer, a Pop-over or the Tour open.

## The Drawer

- drawer/Drawer.tsx slides in from the right, 680 px wide and full width under 700 px, with the breadcrumb "← Cemetery / <name>", the name, version, State badge and scan date, one close button and the tabs Evidence, The fix and History (role tablist, arrow keys). Focus moves to the close button, stays inside and returns to the grave button or List row that opened it. The header is not inert while it is open; the scene, the headline and chips, the Footer bar and the Panel are. A press on the Scan field's place closes it and focuses the field.
- Evidence: the State with its plain sentence (panel/reason-family.ts), every signal with its plain label (drawer/signal-labels.ts), value, source and fetch time, then the missing signals and the policy rules it fails. A source is a link only for an https:// address.
- The fix tab comes from drawer/fix-view.ts: the plan's title, summary and package.json diff, at most one copy button (drawer/clipboard.ts), the notes, and the pull request body in a closed disclosure "Read the pull request text". "Open pull request" sends POST to the API base plus /remediate with { repo, package }, and shows only on a hosted scan whose response has actions.remediate true. The Result area sits outside the scrolling body.
- The PR preview parses with drawer/markdown.ts, which handles exactly what the engine's pr-body.ts emits. A link is made only for an https:// address, with target="_blank" and rel="noopener noreferrer". Output is React elements only.
- History lists the package's Segments newest first. The Drawer always shows the scan date; a note under the scrubber says so at an earlier date.

## Replays, the Guide page, the Legend and the Tour

- A replay is a scan response loaded from /replay/axios.json or /replay/keyv.json on the page origin, by ?replay=axios, ?replay=keyv or the attack picker. Any other replay value loads nothing and shows "That replay does not exist." with "Back to the cemetery".
- replay/ReplayView.tsx takes the screen in place of Home: the breadcrumb "← Cemetery / Real attacks / <package>", the headline and sub-line, the stage with the one grave (drawn at up to REPLAY_SCALE) and the date badge of the Step time, the Step panel ("Step <k> of <m>", the title, the badge, "What happened", "What Sexton shows", Back and "Next step"), the Why card with its https sources and "Reconstructed from public reports. Not a live scan." said once, and a Footer bar with "Play all", one card per Step and "Exit replay". The Step texts come from the replay file; the validator refuses a file whose Step count differs from its Stops or whose Steps share a text. A replay opens at Step 1 and moves only on a press; "Play all" moves one Step every 5,000 ms, restarts from the last Step, and a Step card, Back, "Next step" or "Exit replay" stops it. Each change is announced in a polite live line, never role="status". The replay's grave opens nothing.
- A replay's date badge and Step panel show the Step time; the site footer keeps the replay's window end in a <time> element.
- The Burst plays when a replay arrives at its first Possessed Stop, with no caption; under Reduced motion its final pose shows at once.
- The Guide page (guide/GuidePage.tsx, from guide/guide-content.ts) replaces Home with the two-minute guide; the Legend (header/Legend.tsx) is a Pop-over under its button, outside the page flow, with the five States, the ghost, the pennant, the Not-published mound and the fix order line. Both draw their graves with scene/GravePicture.tsx, which calls the Scene's drawGrave into a small canvas named in words.
- The Tour (tour/Tour.tsx) takes the Home action, switches to the Graveyard view and highlights the Scene, the Inspector and the Scan field in three steps. It dims everything but its target with four panels, blocks presses outside its card, places the card with tour/place-card.ts inside the viewport, keeps focus and Tab in the card and returns focus to the Tour button. The Guide ghost sits beside the step title. It starts only when pressed.
- Jargon in running text and signal labels is a Term (glossary/glossary.ts) shown as a button with a dotted underline; its meaning opens as a Pop-over inside the viewport. No Term is marked inside a button, link, breadcrumb, heading or column heading.

## Addresses and loading

- The View address (load/view-address.ts) names each view: "/" is Home of the Sample scan; repo=, scan=, view=list, grave=, at=, replay=<id> alone and page=guide (with any repo= kept). load/history-step.ts decides each change: a new Source, the Guide page, a Replay, the List switch and the Home action from a Replay or the Guide page push; a Drawer open pushes its own entry and any close but Back goes back over it; the scrubber replaces; a Step, a tab, a filter and a Pop-over leave the address alone. Back and Forward restore the View of their entry. A part the loaded scan or the view does not take is dropped with a replace.
- A fresh repo= link fills the Scan field and shows "Scan this repository" over the Sample scan and sends nothing; a reload of the tab where the visitor started that scan (load/scanned-pref.ts) starts it again. A Live scan shown in the page is held in memory, so Back and Forward show it with no request.
- Web fetches only addresses that pass checkScanUrl in load/use-scan-loader.ts: a scan file on the page's own origin (the demo is /demo/scan.json) or the API base from load/api-base.ts. The API base is a same-origin path such as /api or an https URL. The cache-header behaviour of spec fresh-site stays, with its one retry of a stale static scan.
- A failed load shows one failure card over the Scene (home/FailureCard.tsx): for a Live scan the card of load/scan-failure.ts chosen from the HTTP status and the checked API error code only, with "Try the demo repo" and "Back to the sample"; for the Sample scan, a scan file or a refused replay file, "This scan could not be loaded." with "Try again" and "Back to the sample". Every failure card and the unknown-replay card open with a Dry line, hidden from assistive technology, so the alert announces the plain sentence and the next step, which keep their wording. Only the code field of an error body of at most 4 KiB is read; no other text of an error or its body is shown or logged.

## Words

- A little mischief, never in the way (spec clean-site, requirement 19): the Epitaph lines, the Dry lines and the Scanning line are the exact texts of copy/mischief.ts, and test/mischief.test.tsx fails on a shown line outside it. None names a capability, replaces or shortens a plain sentence or a next step, floats, speaks in a bubble or moves by itself.
- Every sentence the site shows that says what Sexton does or can do is a Claim in copy/claims.ts, tied to the README.md text it rests on; components take Claims by id. test/claims.test.tsx renders every view and fails on a sentence with a capability phrase outside the registry and its listed labels. A new capability sentence goes into the registry with its README text, or README changes first.
- ESLint bans dangerouslySetInnerHTML, innerHTML, outerHTML, insertAdjacentHTML and node: imports in packages/web/src. Render scan text as text only.
- Web imports only types from @sexton/api. Web holds no state rule: the Engine writes Segments into the scan response (timelines) and time/form-at.ts looks up the Segment at the chosen instant.

## The Scene

- Grave form encodes state, nothing else (R-UI-1): a rounded stone with a party hat and flowers for Alive, a tilted stone with a hand clawing out of the dirt for Restless, a cross for Haunted, a cracked tilted stone with a lounging skeleton in a onesie for Dead, an open grave with a dancing devil-costumed zombie and a pink glow for Possessed. Colour follows state. Every grave of one State looks the same; only the animation phase and the Dancer schedule differ.
- A ghost above a grave means one or more advisories. Nothing else is a ghost, with one exception: the Guide ghost (scene/GhostPicture.tsx), the Scene's drawGhost drawn once and still into a 28 px canvas in the Welcome bar and the Tour card's title row, hidden from assistive technology and taking no input. It is never drawn in the Scene or beside a grave picture. The Not-published form is an unmarked mound with no ghost; the pull request marker is a pennant beside the grave, and the grave button name ends in "pull request opened". Both have a Legend entry. The marker comes from the scan response (pullRequests) and from pull requests opened in this session.
- No continuous visual encodings: no tombstone height, no opacity scales, categorical only. The three depth Tiers (scene/tiers.ts): Possessed and Dead in front at scale 1, Haunted and Restless in the middle at 0.8, Alive at the back at 0.64. A grave's Tier follows its scan-date State; rows are centred and spread to fill the width; triage order holds within a Tier and in the keyboard order; grave rectangles never overlap. When the graves do not fit the Scene at full size, all three Tiers shrink together by one fit scale (scene/layout.ts fitScale, 1 down to MIN_FIT_SCALE 0.4), so Home fits the viewport at 1440 by 900, 1366 by 768 and 1280 by 800; a scan that does not fit at 0.4 keeps full size and scrolls. Names keep at least 12 px of font and 36.9 px of width on screen. In the Wide layout the Panel of Home and of a Replay takes the left column's height and scrolls inside itself when its cards are taller (data-scroll), never the page. A Replay's one grave may shrink to 0.8 on a short Scene (REPLAY_MIN_FIT_SCALE; its Scene minimum is 332 px outside the Narrow layout), so both Replays fit at those three sizes too. In the Narrow layout (under 700 px, frontFirst) the Tiers stack front first, two front graves to a row, never under 0.6. The ground reaches the bottom of the Scene card, above the Footer bar.
- Readable without the legend (R-UI-5). If a first-time viewer cannot tell which grave to fix first, the encoding is wrong.
- Order graves by triage: Possessed first, then Dead, Haunted, Restless, Alive, then advisory severity, then transitive reach, then name. Triage order and grave positions always use the scan date; only form, colour, ghost and the inscription version follow the chosen date.
- The scene is a 2D canvas (scene/Scene.tsx); grave buttons in overlay/GraveButtons.tsx give keyboard focus and aria-labels. The hovered or focused grave gets the Lantern glow.
- Scenery is seeded (scene/scenery-seed.ts). No Math.random. Stars, grass tufts and the backdrop (church, tree, fence, bats, fog, vignette, grain, the cratered moon) depend on the width only, never on the scan. The Shadow and the Stone texture use fixed alpha bytes from scene/palette.ts.
- Characters are original vector art (scene/characters/vector.ts) at 12 Poses a second, driven by one clock in Scene.tsx that only repaints the canvas and stops while the page is hidden, or one Pose step per half beat while Music plays. Under prefers-reduced-motion every Character and the bats hold still. A Dead skeleton dances 2 s in every 12 s for one Dead grave in five, chosen from a seed of the package name. The ghost is original art; never draw or embed the Kiro logo. Do not change scene/characters except where a spec says so.
- No grave covers another grave's name. Everything a grave draws but its glow lies in its Art box (scene/art-box.ts). The bounds probe of test/art-box.test.ts draws every Form, Pose, Ghost drift, Burst and Priest phase and fails on a mark outside its box.
- The Halo (scene/halo.ts) is the strong part of the Possessed glow, drawn only where no checked token can sit; haloMask is the one source of its cuts. The selection ring and the focus outline sit on their Ring casing. The pit edge of the open grave is at least 20 CIEDE2000 from the ring gold; the ring is the only gold outline near a grave.
- The burial plays once per package and loaded scan after a pull request is opened: 1200 ms in three phases (the stone sinks, a mound shows, the stone returns with the pennant). Under Reduced motion the Vigil plays in its place. The Priest (scene/characters/priest.ts) walks to the grave and reads. The burial never changes a grave's state, shape or fill.
- The Load moment raises the graves in triage order on the first scan of a page load, within 1,000 ms, never under Reduced motion. The rectangles never move.
- A still grave body, a ghost and a glow are drawn from offscreen sprites (scene/sprites.ts), so 300 graves repaint inside 16 ms locally (50 ms on CI), with a filter and the hover ring too.

## Type, colour, sound and access

- Fonts are self-hosted under public/fonts as woff2 files with their OFL files: Inter for the interface and the canvas name labels (Inter 600), Cormorant Garamond 600 for the wordmark and headlines. Versions, signal keys and diffs use the system monospace stack. No other font ships, and no font request leaves the page origin.
- Every colour is a token in scene/palette.ts; a new token joins the contrast tests (4.5:1 for text on each background it is used on). styles.css holds no colour literal. The palette is night-only. State colours: Alive #8fe3d0, Restless #e69f00, Haunted #56b4e9, Dead #bfb8ad, Possessed #cc79a7, at least 8 apart in CIEDE2000 under simulated colour-vision deficiencies. The five state colours are checked in test/colour-vision.test.ts. The Lantern glow and the Possessed glow are Glow stops in the contrast tests, and a grave has at most one glow; the Halo counts as part of the Possessed glow. The gold accent stays at least 10 from Restless and never colours body text.
- The site makes no generated sound and creates no AudioContext. Music (music/track.ts, music/use-music.ts) is one recorded track in the Track slot: Ghost Party by The Piano Says, CC BY 3.0, 110 BPM, served from public/music on the page origin. The Music button starts off and no audio element exists until the first turn-on; on, the track loops and the Characters dance to its beat, except under prefers-reduced-motion; off, it pauses and the normal idle motion returns. The CSP meta adds media-src 'self' because a track is set. The site footer shows the track's one-line credit in its line; the four credit lines, word for word, are on the Guide page under "Music", in README.md and in CREDITS.md.
- WCAG 2.1 AA. Every grave is reachable by keyboard, prefers-reduced-motion is respected, and axe reports no violation on Home, the List, the Drawer on each tab, a Replay and the Guide page.
- The site offers no GitHub sign-in.
